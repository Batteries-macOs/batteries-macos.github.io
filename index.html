How It Works

Step 1: Enable Wi-Fi Sync

In Finder (Catalina+) or iTunes (Mojave), turn on "Show this device when on Wi-Fi." Your devices now broadcast battery data over the network.

Step 2: Launch and Configure

Open Batteries. Select devices and set low-battery thresholds (e.g., 20%). Toggle notifications as needed.

Step 3: Add to Notification Center

Drag the Batteries widget to your Notification Center. View all levels at a glance.

Step 4: Stay Charged

Devices sync automatically when on the same Wi-Fi. Alerts keep you proactive.

FAQ

Which devices does it support?

Any battery-powered Apple device: iPhone, iPad, MacBook, AirPods, keyboard, mouse, trackpad, and more.

Does it work over the internet?

No—requires same local Wi-Fi network. That's the reliable way to get accurate battery data.

Can I customize alerts?

Yes. Set per-device thresholds or disable entirely in app settings.
